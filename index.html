Rewind Clip Uploader

A private tool for moving short clips onto the channels we run.

What it is

A small program that runs on one computer. It takes a finished video clip and puts it into the drafts of a social account we own, so it is waiting there ready to post. It is not a service, there is no sign-up, and it is not available to anyone else.

How it works

  1. A clip is cut and rendered on the operator's machine.
  2. The tool uploads that file to the connected account's drafts.
  3. The account holder opens the app, looks at the draft, and decides whether to publish it.

The tool never publishes anything. A person makes that call every time.

Accounts it is used with

SpeedRewind, FloridaMadeRewind and Lacy Rewind — all operated by us.
