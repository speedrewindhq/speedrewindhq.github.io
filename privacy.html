Privacy Policy

Rewind Clip Uploader · last updated 07 November 2026

This policy covers Rewind Clip Uploader, a private tool used by one operator to upload video clips to their own social accounts. It has no users other than that operator, and it collects nothing from anyone else.

What is stored

  • An access token for each connected account, so the tool can upload without asking you to sign in every time.
  • The account's user ID and display name, so uploads go to the right account and can be labelled correctly.
  • The video files being uploaded, and a record of which ones have been sent.

Where it is stored

On the operator's own computer, in a folder readable only by that user account. Nothing is stored on a server, nothing is sent to a third party, and there is no database, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

What is not collected

No personal data about TikTok viewers. No contact lists, no browsing history, no location, no advertising identifiers. The tool never reads other people's content.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with anyone. Data goes only between the operator's computer and TikTok's own API.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until they expire or the account is disconnected. Disconnecting the app in your TikTok settings revokes access immediately. Stored tokens and files can be deleted by the operator at any time by removing them from the computer.

Any question about this policy, or a request to delete stored data, can be sent to the address at the foot of this page.
